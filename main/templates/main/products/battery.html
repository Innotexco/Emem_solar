Battery Technology & Features

Advanced lithium iron phosphate chemistry delivers reliability, safety, and years of dependable performance.

LiFePO₄ Chemistry

Superior cycle life of 5,000+ cycles with minimal degradation. Thermally stable and inherently safe for residential and commercial use.

Modular Expansion

Stack multiple units to scale capacity from 5 kWh to 30+ kWh. Grow your storage as your energy needs increase.

Built-in BMS

Integrated battery management system monitors cell voltage, temperature, and state of charge to maximize safety and lifespan.

Smart Monitoring

Real-time mobile app displays state of charge, power flow, efficiency metrics, and historical performance data.

Ideal Battery Applications

Emem batteries power diverse energy strategies for every scenario.

Demand Charge Reduction

Reduce peak consumption fees by storing energy during off-peak hours and discharging during demand spikes. Ideal for commercial buildings and facilities.

Outage Protection

Automatic backup power during grid failures keeps critical loads running. Essential for homes and businesses with sensitive equipment or continuity requirements.

Self-Consumption

Store solar energy during the day and use it in evening hours. Maximize self-consumption rates and minimize grid exports for optimal economics.

Time-of-Use Optimization

Charge during low-rate periods and discharge when rates peak. Maximize savings with time-varying electricity pricing strategies.
