Solar Panel Technology

Advanced cell technologies engineered to maximize sunlight conversion and lifetime energy production.

Monocrystalline Efficiency

Single-crystal silicon delivers 18-22% efficiency. Our premium grade uses passivation and anti-reflective coatings to minimize losses and maximize light absorption even in low-light conditions.

Bifacial Technology

Both front and back surfaces capture sunlight. Bifacial panels generate 10-25% additional energy through reflected light, ideal for ground-mounted systems on light-colored surfaces.

Half-Cut Cells (HJT)

Heterojunction technology with half-cut cells reduces internal losses and improves performance at high temperatures. Up to 23% efficiency with exceptional temperature tolerance.

Durability & Weathering

Tempered glass fronts, aluminum frames, and encapsulation materials withstand salt spray, hail, wind, and UV exposure. IP67 rated for harsh marine and tropical environments.
