Safety Warning

  • • Do not short-circuit the battery terminals.
  • • Do not expose the battery to fire or high temperatures.
  • • Do not puncture, crush, or disassemble the battery.
  • • Do not use if the battery is swollen, leaking, or damaged.
  • • Keep away from children.
  • • Charge and discharge only with a compatible, approved charger and inverter.
  • • Do not immerse in water or allow moisture to enter.
